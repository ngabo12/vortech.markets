Accès aux marchés

Market Access Through HFM

Vortech Markets is an Introducing Broker (IB) for HFM, connecting clients with an established global online trading platform.

A clear and independent relationship

  • Global Market Access Access a broad range of financial instruments through HFM's trading infrastructure.
  • Professional Trading Environment Clients introduced through Vortech can access HFM's available trading platforms, tools and market services.
  • Independent Brokerage Relationship Vortech provides education, market information and client support, while brokerage services are provided directly by HFM.
Learn about HFM Open an account through Vortech

HFM is an independent financial services provider. Vortech Markets operates as an Introducing Broker and may receive compensation for eligible client referrals. Brokerage services, account terms, execution and applicable fees are provided by HFM and are subject to its terms and conditions.
